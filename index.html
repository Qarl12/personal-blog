Career Exploration

Network Security Analyst

Job Responsibilities

  • Monitor and analyse network traffic for suspicious activity and intrusions
  • Conduct vulnerability assessments and penetration testing
  • Respond to security incidents and perform forensic investigations
  • Design and implement firewalls, IDS/IPS, and VPN solutions
  • Develop and enforce security policies and compliance standards
  • Prepare detailed security reports and recommendations for stakeholders

Required Skills

  • Proficiency in network protocols (TCP/IP, DNS, HTTP/S, SMTP)
  • Experience with SIEM tools (Splunk, IBM QRadar)
  • Knowledge of ethical hacking and penetration testing
  • Scripting skills in Python, Bash, or PowerShell
  • Certifications: CompTIA Security+, CEH, CISSP
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving mindset

Relevance to Modern Cybersecurity

In an era where cyber threats evolve daily — from ransomware attacks targeting critical infrastructure to state-sponsored espionage — the role of a Network Security Analyst has never been more vital. Malaysia's digital economy growth, driven by initiatives like MyDIGITAL and the National Cybersecurity Strategy (NACSA), has amplified demand for skilled defenders. Organisations like CyberSecurity Malaysia (CSM) sit at the frontline of this challenge. A Network Security Analyst bridges technical expertise with strategic defence, protecting both data assets and public trust — making it one of the most impactful and future-proof careers in IT.
